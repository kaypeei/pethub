# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Project: PetHub Appointment Booking System

### 1. Executive Summary
The current appointment process is slow, manual, and prone to errors, leading to missed bookings, long wait times, and poor visibility of vet availability. Customers struggle to schedule appointments efficiently, and staff spend unnecessary time coordinating schedules instead of delivering care.

The proposed solution is a streamlined online appointment booking system that automates scheduling, provides real‑time availability, and sends reminders to reduce no‑shows. This digital workflow improves customer experience, reduces administrative workload, and increases operational efficiency across the service.

### 2. Business Objectives
- Improve appointment scheduling efficiency
- Reduce missed appointments
- Enhance customer experience

### 3. Scope
**In Scope:**  
- Online appointment booking  
- Customer notifications  
- Vet availability management  

**Out of Scope:**  
- Payment processing  
- Inventory management  

### 4. Functional Requirements
FR1: The system shall allow customers to book appointments online.  
FR2: The system shall send automated reminders.  
FR3: The system shall allow vets to update availability.

### 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- Performance  
- Security  
- Usability  
- Reliability  

### 6. Assumptions & Constraints
Assumptions
Users have access to a stable internet connection to book appointments online.  
Staff and vets will adopt the new digital workflow with minimal resistance.  
Customer contact details (email/phone) are accurate for sending reminders.  
The organisation has the necessary infrastructure to support a cloud‑based system.  
All stakeholders will be available for requirements validation and UAT sessions.  

Constraints
Budget and timeline limitations may restrict advanced features in the first release.  
Integration with existing systems (e.g., CRM, scheduling tools) may require vendor approval.  
Data protection and NHS/UK GDPR compliance must be maintained at all times.  
System performance must support peak booking periods without degradation.  
Only approved technologies and platforms can be used due to organisational policies.  

### 7. Risks
Key Risks
User adoption may be lower than expected due to resistance to new digital processes.  
Data quality issues (incorrect contact details, incomplete records) may affect reminders and scheduling accuracy.  
Technical integration challenges could delay deployment if existing systems are outdated or undocumented.  
System downtime or performance issues may impact booking availability during peak periods. 
Compliance risks related to data protection (GDPR/NHS standards) if processes are not followed correctly.  

Mitigation Strategies
Provide early stakeholder engagement, training, and clear communication to support adoption. 
Implement data validation rules and periodic data cleansing to improve accuracy.  
Conduct technical feasibility checks and involve IT teams early to reduce integration risks.  
Use load testing, monitoring, and fallback procedures to maintain system reliability.  
Embed privacy‑by‑design principles and regular compliance reviews to ensure regulatory alignment.
